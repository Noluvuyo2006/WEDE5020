## Project Overview
The non-profit organisation is named The Love Trust, located in Midrand, Johannesburg, Gauteng, South Africa. This organisation was founded in 2009 with the mission to reduce poverty and inequality through quality Christian education.  The organisation aims its services towards vulnerable communities, specifically the underprivileged children and women. They later established Nokuphila Pre-primary and Primary School in Tembisa, Gauteng. The Love Trust developed tertiary education and teacher training programmes, specifically for Early Childhood Development practitioners and Primary School educators. Their mission is to provide high-quality, holistic and values-based Christian education to vulnerable children, while training teachers with necessary skills to make a difference in disadvantaged communities. Their vision is to raise a generation of servant leaders and break the cycle of poverty to contribute positively to the community and society. They target vulnerable and disadvantage children in South African communities, as well as existing and aspiring Early Childhood Development practitioners while empowering black women seeking for better employment opportunities. (Trust, no date)
## Webiste Goals and Objectives
The main goal of The Love Trust website is to raise awareness and support for the organisation’s education initiatives and serve as a central hub for engagement with donors, volunteers and partners. Another goal is to increase fundraising opportunities as they rely on financial support to keep the schools and training programmes functioning. The website plays a huge role in promoting the organisation’s mission to ensure the effectiveness it should clearly have defined goals to guide its design, content and functionality. The website needs to increase the number of visitors by improving search engine optimization (SEO), updating blog content regularly and promoting the website on social media platforms. Their target is to increase the number of completed “Contact Us” or “Donation” forms by 15% quarterly. They can achieve this goal by encouraging users to donate, volunteer and partner. Another target is to decrease their bounce rate by 10% by providing clear, accurate and engaging “About Us” information. They should also include success stories, testimonies to build credibility and trust. The website can be evaluated using key performance indicators that measure its effectiveness with achieving its goals. These could include website traffic metrics such as the traffic source, number of users, page views to assess reach and visibility. Conversion metrics like donations, volunteer registrations show how well the website engages with the users. User engagement metrics such as the bounce rate show how users interact with the website. Fundraising KPIs include the total number of donations and measure financial success rate. 
## Key Features and Functionality
The homepage features a hero section with images of happy children at Nokuphila School, Early Childhood practitioners. It includes the latest blog highlights and call-to-action features making navigation easy. The layout is visually appealing and has a fast-loading speed. The About Us page has the mission and vision statements, history of the organisation creating readability for the user, building trust and a well-structured layout for clear and easier understanding. The contact page consists of the contact form for general enquiries, contact details such as their email address and telephone number, and the physical address. There is a programme and service page providing information of the educational programmes, teacher training and their support services offered to the children. The get involved page includes the volunteering opportunities, partnership information.
