The Love Trust


Our Services

Nokuphila School

We provide quality and affordable education to the children.

Early Childhood Development Training

We provide tertiary education and training for educators, more especially Early Childhood Development practitioners and Primary School educators.

Community programmes

We support as well as empower black women seeking for better work opportunities. We strive to reduce poverty in the community and enquality through education, and the educators. We improve the children's nutrition and their psycho-social behaviour.
