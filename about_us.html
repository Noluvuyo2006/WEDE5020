The Love Trust


About us

History

The non-profit organisation is named The Love Trust, located in Midrand, Johannesburg, Gauteng, South Africa. They later established Nokuphila Pre-primary and Primary School in Tembisa, Gauteng. This organisation was founded in 2009 with the mission to reduce poverty and inequality through quality Christian education. The organisation aims its services towards vulnerable communities, specifically the underprivileged children and women. The Love Trust developed tertiary education and teacher training programmes, specifically for Early Childhood Development practitioners and Primary School educators.

Mission

To provide high-quality, holistic and values-based Christian education to vulnerable children, while training teachers with necessary skills to make a difference in disadvantaged communities.

Vision

To raise a generation of servant leaders and break the cycle of poverty to contribute positively to the community and society.
